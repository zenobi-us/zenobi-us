A few people have been asking me this question, so here goes.

For most off the cuff things, I use [pi](https://pi.dev/). 


## Model Choice

My model of choice is GPT 5.3 Codex or GPT 5.3 mini.

If i can get access to Claude, it should be via a provider that doesn't hate me. So usually this means github copilot.

## Harness Choice: Pi

Why pi? because it's the most extensible, most model and provider agnostic.

> It's also not a flicker-fest of epillepsy induced rave mode in your terminal
